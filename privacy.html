Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At ReadFlow, your privacy matters. We believe your reading journey should remain personal, secure and under your control. This Privacy Policy explains how ReadFlow handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

ReadFlow may store information you voluntarily provide, including your name, profile details, books, reading progress, reading sessions, notes, ratings and preferences.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used to provide the features of ReadFlow, manage your personal library, track your reading activity, display statistics and synchronize your data when enabled.

3. Data Storage

ReadFlow stores information locally in your browser. When cloud synchronization is enabled, selected information may also be stored using connected cloud services.

4. Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, ReadFlow uses Google's authorization system to access its private application storage. This connection is used to synchronize your reading data. ReadFlow does not access unrelated files in your Google Drive.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Access to connected services is managed through authentication and authorization mechanisms.

6. Sharing of Information

ReadFlow does not sell your personal information. Your information is not shared with third parties for advertising. Some information may be processed by services required to operate the application.

7. Your Choices

You can manage your reading information, disconnect connected services and export your data using the features available within ReadFlow.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how ReadFlow handles information, please contact the application developer through the official ReadFlow project page.
